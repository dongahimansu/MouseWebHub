DFU Preparation

Connect your mouse, then check that it is ready before continuing to the firmware update.

i

Connect the mouse over WebHID, then check its existing config-channel DFU status. Section 6 unlocks only when the DFU sync response reports the secondary image slot as clean and inactive. This readiness check does not reboot the device or start a separate bootloader.

ⓘ Connect the mouse, then check whether it is ready for an update.
6
Firmware Over-The-Air Update (SMP)

Choose the firmware file for your mouse and verify it before flashing. Keep the mouse connected during the update.

i

Select a signed MCUboot application image (for example, app_update.bin). Verify its header and SHA-256 before upload. MCUboot validates the image signature on the mouse. Use a Web Bluetooth-capable browser over HTTPS or localhost.

ⓘ Check update readiness in section 5 before selecting a firmware file.
